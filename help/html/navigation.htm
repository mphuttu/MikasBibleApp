Navigating the Bible

Opening a Chapter

  1. In the Contents panel on the left, find the book you want to read. All 66 books are listed in canonical order.
  2. Click the expand arrow (►) next to the book name to see its chapters.
  3. Click the chapter you want. The text appears immediately in the right panel.

Reading the Text

Each verse is shown on its own line, prefixed by its verse number. The chapter heading appears in bold at the top of the pane.

Tip: Use the scroll bar on the right edge of the reading pane to move through long chapters.

Copying Text

  1. Click and drag in the reading pane to select one or more verses.
  2. Press Ctrl+C or choose Edit > Copy from the menu to copy the selection to the clipboard.
  3. Paste the text into any other application with Ctrl+V.

Printing a Chapter

  1. Navigate to the chapter you want to print.
  2. Choose File > Print or press Ctrl+P.
  3. Select your printer in the Print dialog and click Print.

The printout includes the book name and chapter number as the document title visible in the print spooler.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Key Action
Ctrl+C Copy selected text
Ctrl+A Select all text in the chapter
Ctrl+P Print current chapter
F1 Open this Help file
